How it works

One engine, four situations: Self, a client or team member, two people, or a group. Scoring, the live read-out, and the report follow from the situation you pick.

  1. Self — a private read on how you come across.
  2. Client or team member — one other person is scored.
  3. Two persons — both people, and the chemistry between them.
  4. Group — how the room moved, read as one conversation.
