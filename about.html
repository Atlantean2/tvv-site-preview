    Michael Calmeyer Hentschel is a strategic venture executive, analyst and change agent. Since 1987 he has led TechVest Ventures, an American accelerator advisory service providing strategic CFO, interim management, business development and consulting support. He is presently strategic CFO of IntualityAI, Bryson Industries, StemXbank and CognomIQ, and is a co-founder and board member of Intuality and CognomIQ.

    His experience spans venture capital, corporate finance, business development, board management and operational strategy. As full-time CFO at Desktone in Boston and at Cyber Resilience in Mountain View, he helped raise and manage $17 million and $10 million respectively, and he has assisted more than 40 venture CEOs to raise more than $140 million in equity. Under the TechVest umbrella alone, after his Schroder Ventures years, he has raised or helped to raise more than $50 million in expansion capital for TechVestVentures clients.

    In the 1980s he co-managed venture funds including the $58 million Schroder Ventures and the $20 million Santa Clara Ventures, with 27% annual IRR over ten years in his deals. Before that he was an investment banker to pre-IPO technology companies with Berkeley Capital.

    He has taught Executive MBAs at Duke-Fuqua and published market research books and five years of the bi-weekly TechVest Econometrics and TechVest Biometrics magazines. He holds a Kellogg MBA from Northwestern in Finance and Marketing, and a cum laude BA in Archaeology and Economics from Yale, and speaks English, German and French. He presently deploys a “staff” of 258 AI Agents for clients, including TechVestVentures itself.
