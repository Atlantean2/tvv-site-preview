Idea Power


There are many ways to create and track idea funnels. Some are regional and take a very large sampling, like www.ideamille.com which seeks to track a thousand good ideas in a geographical region in order to build new entrepreneurial businesses of any type that creates viable and sustainable jobs as well as profit. Some are already highly refined and specialized and drawn through filters of ERP and CRM systems that look for ideas for a specific company to improve their specific operations, like the above illustration and what Novant Health Systems does to evaluate internal and external ideas. The most important filter and element of an idea funnel (and incidentally also of sales funnels) is the discovery or encountering of an internal champion in an organization that will not simply buy a product or service but IMPLEMENT it internally. Proper implementation leads to replication and optimization, and is the essence of good management.

When innovation funnels don’t work (most do not), the fault is in not building in the irreplaceable human champion element.
