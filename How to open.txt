TechVestVentures home page prototype v7.9 2026-11-03 (not published, not on GitHub)

1. Unzip "TVV Home Prototype v7.9 2026-11-03.zip" anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome, Edge or Safari. No internet or install is needed for the pages.
3. Click Ventures, Approach, Thinking or About, in the tabs or in the black cosmos column on the left.
   A transition picture fades in over the page, holds briefly, then dissolves smoothly into the next page, like a
   PowerPoint fade: 0.7 s in, 0.6 s hold, 0.7 s out, 2 seconds in all.
   Each click shows the next of six pictures in turn (Red Arrow Comet first).
   The cosmos column stays still the whole time. Click or press any key during the hold to skip ahead.
4. The site pictures (including the first, TVV Daily Risk Alert Digest) on the Home page and the Ventures tab open the sites in a new tab, with the same kind
   of transition there: the next picture fades in, holds, dissolves to white, then the site opens.
   The Augmentive tile opens the local prototype, so keep the two unzipped folders side by side.

Tips
- To freeze the transition for a close look, add ?preview=transition to the address, e.g. index.html?preview=transition
  (add &tx=1 to &tx=6 to pick one of the six pictures)
- If your computer is set to reduce motion, there is a short fade and no hold, on purpose.
- Text in dashed boxes marked [Placeholder] is not final copy.
- To add art to the cosmos column, see README.txt.
- The zip leaves out the 4K PNG originals of the art (about 50 MB) to stay under 25 MB, so the pages use the
  compressed WebP copies everywhere. If you copy the folder assets/img/art/4k/ from the full working copy into
  the same place, screens 2560 pixels wide or more load the 4K PNGs automatically after the page opens.
