    Michael Calmeyer Hentschel

    Strategic Venture Executive, Analyst, and Change Agent.

    Profile

    Since 1987, Michael has been CEO of TechVest Ventures, an American accelerator advisory service with a current portfolio of 2 private equity co-Founder participations and 3 further project management clients.

    Michael applies deep experience in Venture Capital, Corporate Finance, Business Development, Board Management, and Operational Strategy implementation. Presently strategic CFO of IntualityAI, Bryson Industries, StemXbank, and CognomIQ. Previously, as full-time CFO at Desktone Boston, and full-time CFO at Cyber Resilience Mountain View, he helped raise and manage $17 million and $10 million respectively. He has assisted 40+ venture CEO’s to raise more than $140 million in equity. He presently deploys a “staff” of 258 AI Agents on behalf of various clients including TechVestVentures itself.

    In the 80's, he co-managed VC Funds such as $58-million Schroder Ventures and $20-million Santa Clara Ventures in Silicon Valley, with superior 27% annual IRR returns for ten years in his deals at J Henry Schroder Wagg. Prior to that he was an Investment Banker to pre-IPO technology companies with Berkeley Capital. In addition to a Kellogg MBA and Yale BA, ongoing educational involvements have included teaching Executive MBA’s at Duke-Fuqua, publishing market research books, and five years of the bi-weekly TechVest Econometrics and TechVest Biometrics Magazines. He graduated Cum Laude in Archaeology and Economics from Yale University, is multi-lingual in English, German, and French, and holds an MBA from Kellogg at Northwestern University in Finance, Marketing, and International Business.
