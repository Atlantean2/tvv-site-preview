How to open the Augmentive AI Ventures prototype
1. Unzip the folder anywhere (keep it next to "tvv prototype v3" if you want the link from the TechVestVentures tile to work).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser; no internet or install needed except for the outside links.
3. Click any tab or picture on the left to move between pages. The hands picture appears for 3 seconds between pages; click to skip.
To turn the in-between picture off, open assets/js/config.js in a text editor and change "all" to "off".
